Our Mission

What CTDG Exists To Do

To generate, translate, and apply evidence on environmental and health challenges through research, data, education, and practical solutions—equipping people, communities, and organizations with useful knowledge and capabilities.

🔬 GENERATE Evidence
📖 TRANSLATE Insight

Our Vision

The Future We Want To Build

To build a globally connected and evidence-informed society where environmental and health challenges are understood as interconnected, people and organizations are equipped to respond, and knowledge is transformed into sustainable solutions.

  • ✓ Decisions informed by accessible, credible evidence.
  • ✓ Youth & institutions equipped to investigate & respond.
  • ✓ Tested, measured, and continuously improved community solutions.
CTDG Program Architecture

Our 5 Core Programs

Connecting public awareness, community research, data analytics, youth capacity, and practical interventions.

Program 01

Everyday Actions, Hidden Impacts

Public Education & Behaviour Awareness

Flagship storytelling connecting everyday actions → environmental consequences → health implications → practical responses.

🔥 Starter Topic: Open Burning — The Smoke We Don't See.
Program 02

Environment & Health Pulse

Community Research & Local Data

Recurring community surveys, street interviews, and observations documenting local environmental and health realities.

📹 Outputs: Community Pulse Datasets & Video Reports.
Program 03

CTDG Evidence Lab

Research, Data & Innovation

The analytical engine turning datasets into credible evidence briefs, data stories, and concise research PDF reports.

📊 Outputs: Research Briefs & Data Dashboards.
Program 04

CTDG Youth Impact Lab

Youth Research, Skills & Leadership

Equipping young people with research methods, survey design, data analysis, and investigative reporting tools.

🎓 Outputs: Trained Cohorts & Youth Portfolios.
Program 05

Community Solutions Lab

Sustainable Livelihoods & Policy Advocacy

Designing economic resilience initiatives and engaging stakeholders to advocate for equitable environmental and public health policies.

🌱 Outputs: Policy Briefs & Local Resilience Plans.
